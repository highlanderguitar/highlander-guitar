The Joe Pass ii–V default is preserved: where ii precedes V, the traversal is evaluated across one dominant region. No separate ii phrase was preferred in this slice; a genuinely ii-specific version would be labeled `SPECIALIZED ii OPTION`.

## Restricted opportunities

- Trail of Tears and Southern Flavor have excellent B7 duration, but only a minor-tonic target is valid.
- Bright Sunny South remains review-only because Dsus2 does not authorize a major-third treatment.
- Farewell Blues needs the D/D♯ split and function confirmed.
- Sitting on Top of the World needs approval of the exact split beat and a shortened two-beat traversal.
- Dear Old Dixie's latest phrase-review-derived rhythm ends before the full guide form; the M20 review uses the cleaned canonical form and needs user confirmation.

Fourteen per-tune TGs place one concise traversal marker on a separate review track and leave the canonical tracks unchanged. Detailed fields and restrictions are in `bh_traversals_setlist_opportunities.csv`.
